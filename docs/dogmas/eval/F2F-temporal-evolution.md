# F2F — Temporal evolution evaluation

**Owner:** whoever used the taxonomy over a period (≥3 months or ≥10 projects).
**Goal:** detect whether evolution of the domain, tooling, or practice requires changes to the taxonomy.
**Time:** ~30-45 minutes.
**Instructions:** based on your real usage, answer per category.

---
